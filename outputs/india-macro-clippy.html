01

RBI finalises Basel III market-risk capital rules for banks

The RBI has issued final market-risk capital directions for commercial banks, adopting a simplified standardised approach under revised Basel III rules. They take effect on 1 April 2027 after a transition period already in place.

Why it matters
The directions change how banks calculate capital against trading-book, foreign-exchange, fund and credit-derivative exposures. Bank disclosures and implementation guidance before April 2027 will show where capital use or portfolio systems need adjustment.

02

Proprietary traders retreat from NSE derivatives

Proprietary traders’ share of NSE equity-derivatives turnover has dropped to its lowest level in almost four years, with closing-auction problems adding to a more restrictive trading environment.

Why it matters
A smaller proprietary-trading presence can alter the mix of liquidity and price discovery in a derivatives market worth about $2 trillion. The next signal is whether the exchange resolves the closing-auction issue and whether participation and turnover stabilise.

India Tech

Adoption, infrastructure, builders

03

Government pushes data centres nearer to power generation

Microsoft has opened its fourth India cloud region in Hyderabad as part of its $20.5 billion commitment to India’s digital and AI infrastructure; the government wants new data centres located closer to generation sites.

Why it matters
Siting large data centres near generation can reduce grid-connection and transmission pressure as AI infrastructure expands. The decision point is whether future project approvals translate that preference into specific power and location commitments.

RSS · YourStory, 21 Sep · 11:29 UTC

04

Meta’s recommendations put safe-harbour protections under scrutiny

Fresh scrutiny of Meta’s intermediary status asks whether recommendation algorithms can affect a platform’s safe-harbour protection under Indian law.

Why it matters
Treating recommendations as active involvement rather than passive hosting could increase platforms’ exposure for the content they amplify. The next observable signal is how a court or regulator defines that line and whether it changes moderation or ranking practices.

RSS · MediaNama, 21 Sep · 07:55 UTC

05

Microsoft’s India South Central region targets zero-water cooling

Microsoft says its new India South Central cloud region is built for AI workloads from day one and will use effectively zero water for cooling.

Why it matters
Water-light cooling can ease a resource constraint for high-density AI infrastructure in India’s expanding cloud footprint. Customer uptake of the region and details of its operating water use will test whether the design delivers at scale.
