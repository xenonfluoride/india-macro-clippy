Mobile phone retailers to protest against UPI MDR charges on Oct 2

Mobile-phone retailers say a proposed 0.4% UPI merchant-discount rate would cost small retailers about ₹40 crore a month and plan an October 2 protest.

Why it matters
A merchant-discount fee would create a funding pool for UPI’s cybersecurity, capacity, and fraud-control costs instead of leaving them entirely to participating institutions.
