Rupee, bonds witness sell-off as crude oil, US Treasury yields rise

The rupee settled at 95.96 per dollar, while the benchmark 10-year government bond yield rose 6 basis points to 7.11 per cent, its highest level since May 21.

Why it matters
Higher crude prices raise India’s dollar import bill while rising U.S. yields can pull capital away from rupee assets, pressuring both the currency and domestic borrowing costs. Watch RBI action, foreign debt flows, and the next oil move to see whether the sell-off becomes a broader external-financing stress.
