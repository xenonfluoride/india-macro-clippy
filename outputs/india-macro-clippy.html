01

12% deficit: Rain report gives a dry reading

A 12% rain shortfall is already lifting pulse prices alongside festive demand, while drought declarations are spreading across Maharashtra and Karnataka.

Why it matters
A weaker harvest pushes food inflation through crop availability while relief spending shifts part of the loss to state budgets. Watch crop-damage estimates and pulse prices to gauge the near-term pressure on household budgets and fiscal support.

02

Russia remains India's top crude supplier in September amid supply squeeze

Russian supplies averaged 1.74 million barrels a day to India through 27 September, as constrained West Asian availability narrowed refiners’ alternatives.

Why it matters
The supply mix preserves a lower-cost crude option when tighter West Asian availability raises refiners’ import vulnerability. Watch October cargo flows, discounts, and any sanctions-enforcement changes for the durability of that buffer.

03

HCLTech software unit to acquire European firm Robotiq.ai for 9 million euros

HCLTech’s Austrian unit will buy 100% of Robotiq.ai for €9 million in cash, subject to the usual net-debt and working-capital adjustments.

Why it matters
The acquisition adds robotic-process-automation capability to HCLTech’s software stack, extending automation into enterprise systems with limited API access. Watch post-close product integration and customer deployments to see whether the deal becomes a meaningful software capability rather than a small bolt-on.

04

SC Refuses To Stay UPI MDR, Seeks Centre’s Response On ₹2,000 Threshold

The Supreme Court declined to pause the Centre’s MDR decision for selected UPI transactions and sought its response on the ₹2,000 threshold.

Why it matters
A charge above a defined threshold would shift part of UPI’s operating cost toward merchants while creating a funding stream for the payments rail. Watch the Centre’s submission and the eventual threshold design, then merchant acceptance, for the adoption-versus-sustainability trade-off.
