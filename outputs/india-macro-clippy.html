01

US exempts select Indian specialty drugs, ingredients from 100% pharma tariff

The United States has exempted eligible Indian specialty medicines, including rare-disease and nuclear-medicine products, from its new pharmaceutical tariff from 29 September.

Why it matters
The exemption keeps a defined group of Indian drug exports out of the new tariff cost, preserving their access to a US market where urgent-health imports need Commerce approval. Watch the eligible-product list and approval flow to see how much trade is covered and which suppliers can use it.

Uttar Pradesh's ethanol supply chain attracts investments in distilleries

Uttar Pradesh is attracting over ₹1,000 crore in private investment from distillers, while rising ethanol output and excise exports are strengthening the state's sugarcane value chain.

Why it matters
More distillery investment ties the sugarcane chain more closely to ethanol output and creates a commercial outlet beyond sugar sales. Watch whether the announced capital is commissioned and whether ethanol output and excise exports continue to rise.

03

Over 40 modules of Shahzad Bhatti network busted, 350 held across 15 States: Amit Shah

The Centre has declared the Shahzad Bhatti network a terrorist organisation, alleging its involvement in cross-border weapons, explosives and narcotics smuggling; authorities say more than 40 modules were dismantled across 15 States.

Why it matters
The designation and arrests widen the state’s ability to disrupt a network alleged to move weapons, explosives and narcotics across borders. Watch the charges, asset actions and any cross-border enforcement detail for evidence of its operational reach.

UPI MDR: SC seeks responses from Centre, RBI

The Supreme Court has sought responses from the Centre and RBI on the proposed UPI merchant-discount rate, while declining an interim stay on the decision.

Why it matters
The case puts the financing of UPI’s merchant acceptance and operating costs under judicial and policy scrutiny while a charge remains live. Watch the Centre and RBI filings, then any NPCI guidance, for the design that would determine merchant cost and payment-rail funding.

HCLTech software unit to acquire European firm Robotiq.ai for 9 million euros

HCLTech’s Austrian software subsidiary will buy all of Robotiq.ai for €9 million, subject to customary net-debt and working-capital adjustments.

Why it matters
