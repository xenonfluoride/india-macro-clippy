01

Gross GST collections rise 23% to ₹2.31 trillion in October on strong sales

Government data released on Sunday showed October gross GST collections rose 23% to more than ₹2.31 lakh crore. Collections from domestic sales rose 16% to more than ₹1.59 lakh crore, while import revenue rose 42% to ₹72,246 crore. After refunds, net GST collections rose 26% to more than ₹2.03 lakh crore.

Why it matters
Higher receipts expand fiscal resources, although nominal tax growth combines demand, prices and the composition of taxable activity. Watch whether domestic collections and net receipts sustain their pace after the festive period.

02

Centre releases addl instalment of tax devolution of ₹1.09 trn to states

The finance ministry said the Centre released an additional ₹1,09,019 crore in tax devolution to states on Saturday, beyond regular monthly devolution. It said the release was intended to help states accelerate capital spending and finance development or welfare expenditure ahead of the festive season.

Why it matters
An extra instalment brings cash forward for state budgets and can ease near-term financing constraints. Watch whether state expenditure data show faster capital spending after the additional transfer.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 31 Oct · 16:25 UTC

03

Govt raises export duty on diesel, aviation fuel; scraps petrol levy

The government raised diesel export duty by ₹3 to ₹11.50 per litre and increased the aviation turbine fuel levy to ₹11 per litre. It also withdrew the ₹2.50 duty on petrol exports.

Why it matters
The revised levies change relative export margins across refined fuels and may influence refiners’ product choices. Watch how refiners adjust export volumes and product mix under the new duty schedule.

New aviation rules mandate upfront disclosure of cancellation charges, refund terms

New aviation rules mandate upfront disclosure of cancellation charges and refund terms. Under Rule 192 of the Bharatiya Vayuyan Niyam, 2026, airlines will have to show passengers the total amount payable on booking websites and tickets.

Why it matters
Making total fares and exit terms visible can reduce the cost of comparing airline bookings. Watch how airlines present these charges across booking pages and issued tickets.

Don’t cover up court failures by freeing convicts, SC tells courts

The Supreme Court has cautioned courts against suspending the sentences of people convicted of heinous offences merely because their appeals are unlikely to be heard in the near future.

Why it matters
The ruling narrows the use of appeal delays alone as a basis for suspending sentences in heinous-offence cases. Watch how appellate courts distinguish delay alone from other grounds when deciding sentence-suspension requests.

Mansukh Mandaviya rolls out EPFO services on WhatsApp, starts auto-settlement of small inoperative accounts

Labour minister Mansukh Mandaviya launched EPFO services through WhatsApp, allowing subscribers to check provident-fund balances and transaction histories. Automatic settlement for small inoperative EPF accounts was also introduced, with around one lakh accounts expected to benefit.

Why it matters
Digital account access and automatic settlement can reduce the administrative effort needed to use provident-fund services. Watch actual settlement completions and subscriber use of the new WhatsApp services.
