01

EPFO approves periodic PF payouts for retirees, eases withdrawal norms

EPFO has approved periodic provident-fund payouts for retirees, Hindustan Times reports. The revised rules reduce the minimum balance members must retain from 25% to 10% of their contributions.

Why it matters
More flexible withdrawals can help retirees spread cash receipts over time while reducing the savings that must remain in their accounts. Watch for implementation instructions explaining payout frequency and how members choose their withdrawals.

02

India's crude oil output likely to fall 20% over next decade: IEA

The IEA forecasts a 20% decline in India's crude oil output over the next decade, Business Standard reports. It expects about 1 million barrels per day of additional refining capacity even as domestic crude production declines, increasing reliance on imported oil.

Why it matters
More refining capacity alongside less domestic crude could increase India's exposure to imported feedstock costs and shipping reliability. Watch whether actual refinery commissioning and domestic production data track the forecast import requirement.

03

Govt proposes major CSR rule changes, seeks to include mental health, clean energy, PM internship scheme

The government has proposed changes to corporate social responsibility rules that would include mental health, clean energy and the PM internship scheme, Hindustan Times reports. The proposals also cover bans on one-off events and product freebies, alongside stricter NGO oversight.

Why it matters
Revising eligible activities and oversight could redirect corporate social budgets and alter compliance work for companies and implementing organisations. Watch the final rules for which proposed activities and restrictions survive into enforceable requirements.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 19 Nov · 14:34 UTC

04

Centre extends PUC validity to 3 years for new BS-VI vehicles, cuts it for BS-IV

Under amended rules, private BS-VI vehicles less than six years old need a Pollution Under Control certificate every three years, Hindustan Times reports. The Centre has also shortened validity for BS-IV vehicles.

Why it matters
Differentiating testing intervals by vehicle category changes owners' recurring compliance burden without establishing that actual emissions have improved. Watch for implementation notices clarifying renewal dates and the testing interval that applies to each vehicle category.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 19 Nov · 09:40 UTC

05

Cyient Semiconductors gets $10 mn strategic investment from GlobalFoundries

Cyient Semiconductors has secured a $10 million strategic investment from GlobalFoundries, Business Standard reports. The transaction values Cyient Semiconductors at $490 million before the investment and will support expansion of its custom-chip, silicon-development and power-semiconductor capabilities.

Why it matters
Strategic capital could help Cyient broaden chip-design capabilities, with commercial value depending on translating development work into customer programmes. Watch for customer design wins and disclosed progress in the capabilities the investment is intended to expand.

RSS · Business Standard Companies, 19 Nov · 05:22 UTC

06

Disseqt AI raises $8M to build enterprise AI safeguards

Disseqt AI has raised $8 million to build enterprise AI safeguards, YourStory reports. The funds will support global customer expansion, and the company is hiring in India across sales, operations and technology while planning expansion into London.

Why it matters
Funding and Indian hiring could expand the company's capacity to supply enterprise AI safeguards, although financing alone does not demonstrate their effectiveness. Watch for disclosed enterprise deployments and evidence that customer use converts the funded expansion into recurring business.
