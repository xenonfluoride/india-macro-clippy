Macro

Policy, prices, external balance

01

CPSEs achieve 60% of FY27 capital expenditure target in April-October

Business Standard reports that capital expenditure by 63 CPSEs rose nearly 13 per cent to ₹5.03 trillion during April–October, with railways, highways and energy companies among the leading spenders.

Why it matters
Executed public-sector investment can support infrastructure suppliers and construction demand before new assets begin operating. Watch whether subsequent spending reports sustain this pace and translate expenditure into completed projects.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 09 Nov · 13:03 UTC

02

India's oilmeal exports fall 28.5% as soymeal loses to Brazil, Argentina

The Solvent Extractors’ Association of India said oilmeal exports fell 28.51 per cent to 12.82 lakh tonnes in April–August of the current fiscal year, against 17.93 lakh tonnes a year earlier. Soybean meal exports fell 73 per cent to 205,000 tonnes from 757,000 tonnes.

Why it matters
Weaker export competitiveness can squeeze Indian processors even when domestic feed demand supports sales. Watch whether the next export data show soybean meal recovering volumes as the international price gap changes.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 09 Nov · 07:17 UTC

03

Govt extends deepsea oil, gas block bidding deadline again to December 10

Business Standard reports that the government extended bidding for deepwater and ultra-deepwater blocks under the tenth and eleventh Open Acreage Licensing Policy rounds to December 10, 2026. The report describes this as the seventh postponement for the tenth round and the third for the eleventh; DGH did not specify the reasons.

Why it matters
More time gives explorers additional room to prepare offers while delaying the path from acreage allocation to drilling. Watch whether bids arrive by the revised deadline or another postponement follows.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 09 Nov · 08:24 UTC

National & Strategy

Statecraft, law, security, foreign affairs

04

DST: No INSPIRE grant if student fails, misses exam

Hindustan Times reports that students who fail or miss a university examination will no longer retain scholarships under the Centre’s INSPIRE-Scholarship for Higher Education scheme. The annual research mentorship grant is now restricted to the third and fourth years, reducing maximum support over five years.

Why it matters
Tighter retention and mentorship rules can reduce financial continuity for students pursuing science education. Watch how implementation guidance handles interrupted examinations and communicates the revised support schedule.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 10 Nov · 02:20 UTC

05

SC directs Uttar Pradesh to identify undertrial prisoners languishing in jails despite getting bail

Hindustan Times reports that the Supreme Court directed Uttar Pradesh to identify undertrial prisoners still in jail despite being granted bail, in consultation with DLSA.

Why it matters
Identifying prisoners who remain detained after bail can expose practical barriers between a court order and release. Watch whether the identification exercise produces documented releases and explanations for continuing detention.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 09 Nov · 10:17 UTC

India Tech

Adoption, infrastructure, builders

06

Number of villages without 4G connectivity falls by three-fourths in four years

The Hindu reports that the number of villages without 4G connectivity fell by three-fourths in four years. Its report draws on data obtained under the Right to Information Act.

Why it matters
A smaller village coverage gap can widen access to digital services, although coverage alone does not establish reliable household use. Watch whether subsequent coverage data also document usable speeds and service reliability in the remaining gaps.

RSS · The Hindu Technology, 09 Nov · 12:44 UTC

07

Enterprise data intelligence platform SplashBI opens GCC in Hyderabad, to invest over $100 million

Economic Times reports that SplashBI opened a global capability centre in Hyderabad. A company release says it plans to invest over $100 million over five to seven years in infrastructure, technology and talent to scale the centre and strengthen global product engineering and delivery.

Why it matters
An expanded Hyderabad engineering base could deepen local enterprise-software skills if the planned investment is executed. Watch for disclosed staffing and infrastructure additions that demonstrate delivery against the investment plan.

RSS · Economic Times Tech, 09 Nov · 11:46 UTC
