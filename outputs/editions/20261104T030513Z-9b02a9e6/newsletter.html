01

India eases forex reporting rules for small value service exports, imports

The Reserve Bank of India has eased foreign exchange reporting for services exports and imports, reducing compliance requirements for resident individuals and setting a ₹1 million threshold for certain transactions. Resident individuals exporting services will be deemed to have filed export declarations after submitting payment details to banks; reporting obligations for larger transactions and non-individual entities continue.

Why it matters
Simpler reporting can reduce paperwork for individual service exporters while leaving banks responsible for collecting payment details. Watch how banks apply the threshold and distinguish individual exporters from entities with continuing reporting duties.

02

Centre plans to expand import clearance gateways for drugs, medical devices

The Health Ministry proposes adding seven inland container depots, a special economic zone and Jewar airport to the approved import gateway list for drugs, medical devices and cosmetics, Business Standard reports.

Why it matters
More approved entry points could reduce routing and clearance bottlenecks for medical imports if the proposal is adopted. Watch for the final approved list and the operational readiness of inspection and clearance services at the added gateways.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 03 Nov · 15:04 UTC

03

Odisha draws ₹1.17 trillion clean energy investment proposals at summit

Odisha signed clean-energy agreements with 15 companies at a renewable energy summit, drawing ₹1.17 trillion in investment proposals for projects spanning 11.74 GW of planned capacity, Business Standard reports.

Why it matters
The proposed generation buildout could widen Odisha's clean-power supply, with the benefit dependent on projects moving from agreements to financed construction. Watch for financial commitments, project clearances and commissioning schedules that convert the summit proposals into operating capacity.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 03 Nov · 14:51 UTC

04

No individual, group can occupy a designated protest site indefinitely: Supreme Court

The Supreme Court said no individual or group can occupy a designated protest site indefinitely, Hindustan Times reports. The observations came while the court heard petitions arising from police action during July protests led by the Cockroach Janata Party.

Why it matters
A limit on indefinite occupation places the duration and shared use of designated protest spaces at the centre of the rights-management tradeoff. Watch for the operative directions and how they distinguish limits on occupation from access to peaceful protest.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 03 Nov · 08:39 UTC

05

VB-G RAM G grievance redressal rules set 15-day deadline

The VB-G RAM G grievance redressal rules set a 15-day deadline and establish mechanisms at gram panchayat, block and district levels, The Economic Times reports. Designated officers will receive, register, inquire into and dispose of complaints; inquiries must be completed within seven working days.

Why it matters
Designated officers and time limits can make rural complaints easier to track through successive levels of administration. Watch whether states appoint accessible officers and report inquiry completion separately from final complaint disposal.

RSS · Economic Times Economy, 02 Nov · 19:12 UTC

06

MeitY unveils indigenous AI Models EKA-1, Dhīr 2B and 5B under IndiaAI mission

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology unveiled EKA-1, Dhīr 2B and Dhīr 5B, three indigenous AI foundation models developed by domestic companies with IndiaAI Mission support, Mint reports. The government highlighted their potential for specialised applications and deployment in secure, offline environments.

Why it matters
The prospect of domestic models for offline use could broaden deployment options for organisations that need tighter control over their data environments. Watch for demonstrated performance on specialised tasks and evidence of secure deployments beyond the unveiling.

RSS · Mint Technology, 03 Nov · 15:40 UTC

07

India's data centre capacity set for 5x surge by 2030: Crisil

Crisil forecasts a fivefold rise in India's data-centre capacity by 2030, YourStory reports. Growth will also depend on timely power supply, regulatory clearances and project execution capabilities.

Why it matters
Projected computing capacity creates demand for reliable electricity and project delivery before it can become usable digital infrastructure. Watch commissioned capacity and power availability against the forecast, rather than counting announced projects as completed supply.

RSS · YourStory, 03 Nov · 12:01 UTC
