Govt projects Kharif foodgrain production at 167.14 mln tonnes despite El Nino; 2025-26 crop output a record

Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan announced a projection of 167.14 million tonnes of kharif foodgrains for the 2026–27 crop year amid a delayed monsoon and El Niño.

Why it matters
Expected harvest volumes guide procurement and inventory planning before the final crop tally. Watch whether the next official estimate revises the projected volume as harvest data come in.

02

RBI accepts ₹8,003 crore of state bonds in its first switch auction

The RBI accepted ₹8,003 crore of state bonds at its first switch auction. Tamil Nadu accounted for the largest share of accepted securities, and limited holdings of eligible bonds at banks constrained participation.

Why it matters
Exchanging debt across maturities gives states a way to spread out repayments that would otherwise fall together. Watch the bonds offered and accepted at the next auction to see whether participation broadens.

03

HC to hear Tata Trusts' plea against Chandra's reappointment on Friday

The Sir Dorabji Tata Trust and the R.D. Tata Trust filed suits in the Bombay High Court challenging N Chandrasekaran’s reappointment as Tata Sons executive chairman for a third five-year term. The trusts allege the September 17 board resolution lacked an affirmative vote required by the company’s articles.

Why it matters
The dispute tests how the holding company’s approval rules apply to the chairman’s appointment. Watch whether the court grants interim relief and specifies which board decisions it would cover.

Supreme Court tightens food labelling norms, gives FSSAI four months to finalise them

The Supreme Court gave the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India four months to finalise tighter food warning labels. The court said delays would come at a cost to public health, especially for children.

Why it matters
A rulemaking deadline gives food makers a timeline for planning packaging changes as the labelling requirements take shape. Watch the regulator’s final text for the products covered and the time manufacturers get to comply.

06

Ultraviolette breaks ground on ₹779 crore electric vehicle plant in Hosur

Ultraviolette broke ground on a ₹779 crore electric vehicle plant in Hosur. The plant is designed for initial annual capacity of 250,000 vehicles, scalable to 500,000, and will make the company’s upcoming electric scooters and motorcycles.

Why it matters
Making scooters and motorcycles at one site could spread factory costs across more vehicles if sales support higher output. Watch for a commissioning date and the first production guidance for each vehicle line.

07

Vi opts against Mumbai spectrum renewal, seeks extension in other circles

Vodafone Idea will forgo renewal of 4.4 MHz of 1800 MHz spectrum in Mumbai and has asked for extensions in other circles until the next spectrum auction.

Why it matters
Letting the license expire reduces the spectrum available to Vodafone Idea in Mumbai and could require it to move traffic onto its remaining bands. Watch how the company plans to serve Mumbai traffic and whether authorities grant extensions in the other circles.
