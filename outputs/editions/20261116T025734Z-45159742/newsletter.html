01

Policy support, exporter resilience drive strong Oct trade performance: CII

Business Standard reported that India’s October goods exports rose 45% year over year to $49.47 billion, while imports rose 16% to $89.35 billion. The goods trade deficit widened to a high for the year of $39.88 billion.

Why it matters
A larger import bill can widen the goods deficit even when exports grow faster in percentage terms. Watch the next trade release for whether the gap narrows and whether export growth extends beyond October.

Mahanadi Coalfields set a price band of ₹129–136 a share for its IPO, which opens on November 19 and closes on November 23. Coal India is selling a 10% stake and will receive the proceeds.

Why it matters
The offer would give public investors direct exposure to the subsidiary and convert part of Coal India’s holding into cash. Watch the final issue price and subscription levels for a measure of investor demand.

Escorts Kubota plans new UP plant with 60,000-tractor capacity: CFO

Escorts Kubota plans to start building a tractor plant near Jewar airport in Uttar Pradesh early next fiscal year, CFO Bharat Madan told PTI. The first phase would have capacity for 60,000 tractors, with room to expand to 100,000.

Why it matters
Added capacity would give Escorts Kubota more room to meet seasonal peaks in tractor demand. Watch for work to begin at the Jewar site early next fiscal year.

US FAA begins 5-day audit of DGCA’s safety oversight system

The US Federal Aviation Administration began an audit of India’s Directorate General of Civil Aviation under its International Aviation Safety Assessment programme. The assessment is scheduled for November 16–20.

Why it matters
The audit scrutinises the regulator’s safety oversight and could identify where it needs to change its practices. Watch for the findings and any corrective actions requested of the DGCA.

Assam to restrict construction in flood-prone areas under proposed zoning law: CM

Assam’s government has prepared a bill that would restrict construction in flood-prone areas. The Assam Flood Plain Zoning Bill is due to be tabled in the state assembly’s winter session next month.

Why it matters
Flood-plain zoning could limit new construction where it would add to flood exposure. Watch the bill as introduced for how it defines flood-prone areas and which construction it would restrict.

Sebi to issue AI usage guidelines for capital markets, focus on investor protection

SEBI plans to issue guidance on AI use in capital markets. The regulator’s chief said the proposed safeguards would cover disclosures, accountability for regulated entities and human involvement in decisions.

Why it matters
Human review could give regulated firms a way to catch errors before automated decisions reach investors. Watch the published text for which uses require human review and what firms must disclose.
