Economic activity growth hits 36-month high of 12.8% in October: ICRA

ICRA said economic activity growth reached 12.8% in October, a 36-month high on its measure. The rating agency said a low base partly drove the rise and that 11 of 16 high-frequency indicators improved.

Why it matters
Improvement across more than half the indicators suggests the pickup extended beyond a few series, although the low base helped lift the headline rate. Watch whether the next reading shows improvement across a similar share of indicators.

Meity revises ‘Made in India’ rules for govt IT hardware purchases

MeitY revised the rules for government purchases of IT hardware to reward local research and development, design and chip manufacturing.

Why it matters
The procurement criteria give vendors a reason to move more design and component work into India when competing for government orders. Look for tender documents that spell out how buyers will score domestic design and chipmaking.

India, UK working to resolve technical glitch over Scotch whisky duty concessions under trade pact

The Commerce Ministry is working with the Finance Ministry, importers and UK authorities to resolve a customs classification issue affecting Scotch whisky duty concessions under the India–UK trade pact. Commerce Secretary Rajesh Agarwal has urged the DRI to release pending consignments.

Why it matters
The classification determines which duty applies, so a customs resolution could affect both import costs and the time shipments spend awaiting clearance. Look for written customs guidance or confirmation that the pending consignments have been released.

‘IDBI Bank’ not ‘State’ under Article 12, rules Supreme Court; dismisses appeal by association | What we know

The Supreme Court held that IDBI Bank falls outside the definition of State under Article 12 of the Constitution and dismissed appeals by its officers’ association.

Why it matters
The classification affects which constitutional remedies officers can seek against the bank in employment disputes. Watch which legal routes officers pursue in any subsequent benefits disputes.

Fossil fuel divide fractures consensus at climate talks in Antalya

Countries at the UN climate summit in Antalya failed to agree on reaffirming the commitment to move away from fossil fuels. India challenged the push for new targets without addressing historical use of the carbon budget and who would pay for the transition.

Why it matters
An agreement on who finances the transition could give India and other developing countries more room to accept common targets. Watch whether the next negotiating text pairs fossil-fuel language with a concrete financing commitment.

AI agents set to reshape Indian workplaces as 93% of leaders plan adoption: KPMG-CII report

A report by KPMG in India and the Confederation of Indian Industry found that 93% of leaders intend to use AI agents to expand what their workforce can do and 59% already use agents to automate workstreams or business processes.

Why it matters
Automating individual processes gives companies a way to test AI agents before expanding their use across teams. Watch whether subsequent employer surveys report more use in day-to-day operations and track the resulting changes in work.

Shadowfax To Acquire WeFast To Expand Into Intra-City Deliveries

Inc42 reported a ₹23.3 crore deal for Shadowfax to acquire the Indian entity of Borzo, the same-day intra-city delivery platform formerly known as WeFast.

Why it matters
Adding a same-day intra-city network could help Shadowfax serve merchants whose orders need to move within a single city. Watch for Shadowfax to announce which cities and merchant services it will add through the deal.
