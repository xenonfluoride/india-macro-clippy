Retail inflation at 6.09% in October, breaches RBI's upper band after 2 yrs

India’s retail inflation reached 6.09% in October, breaching the RBI’s upper band for the first time in two years. Food inflation rose to 8.94% from 7.74% in September.

Why it matters
Higher food prices squeeze household budgets and can complicate the central bank’s rate decision. Watch the November CPI release for signs that food inflation is easing.

02

Nearly 70% of PLI scheme segments on track, says NITI Aayog report

NITI Aayog’s assessment found that nearly 70% of targeted production-linked incentive segments were broadly on track. It reported cumulative employment of 849,069 and incentive disbursals of ₹35,354 crore as of March 2026.

Why it matters
Comparing employment and production across sectors can show whether the same incentive design delivers both outcomes. Watch whether ministries change sector targets or payment criteria in response to the assessment.

03

Govt prohibits lithium-ion battery black mass exports

The government has prohibited exports of lithium-ion battery black mass with immediate effect to retain critical minerals for domestic recycling, an official said.

Why it matters
Keeping black mass in India could increase the feedstock available to domestic battery recyclers. Watch whether recyclers take on more domestic material or exporters report stock building up.

04

Stand-Up India and Mudra Yojana extended by Union Cabinet for 5 more years

The Union Cabinet extended Stand-Up India and Mudra Yojana by five years. It allocated ₹4,111 crore for the Stand-Up India fund and ₹5,000 crore for the Micro Units fund under the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana.

Why it matters
The extensions keep two financing channels available to entrepreneurs and micro businesses as lenders decide how much credit to extend. Watch the loan sanction and disbursement figures for both schemes after the extension.

05

Draft electoral rolls for SSR published in 13 states, 3 UTs; UP to release on Nov 17

Draft electoral rolls were published in 13 states and three Union territories, with Uttar Pradesh due to release its draft on November 17. Bihar’s draft adds about 1.3 million electors over its post-revision roll, while Tamil Nadu’s total rises to 57.7 million and remains below its pre-revision count.

Why it matters
Publishing the drafts gives voters a chance to check their entries and seek corrections before the rolls are finalised. Watch the Uttar Pradesh release and the number of claims and objections recorded before finalisation.

06

CCPA orders Ola to stop advance tipping feature, imposes ₹10 lakh fine

The CCPA said Ola’s advance tipping prompt was misleading and pressured users to pay extra before ride confirmation. It ordered Ola to remove the feature and imposed a ₹10 lakh fine.

Why it matters
Dropping the advance prompt could reduce pressure to pay extra before a ride is confirmed. Watch whether Ola removes the prompt from the booking flow and how the regulator confirms compliance.

OpenAI now allows enterprises to process AI requests within India

OpenAI now lets enterprises process API requests and generate AI responses within India, initially for GPT-6 Luna. The feature is intended to address enterprise data localisation requirements.

Why it matters
Local inference could make the API usable for workloads that require processing to stay within India. Watch whether OpenAI adds more models and whether Indian enterprises report using local inference in production.
