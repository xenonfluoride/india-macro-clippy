Manufacturing PMI dips in October as output, orders see slow growth

HSBC’s India Manufacturing PMI fell to 54.7 in October from 55.1 in September. Export orders grew at their fastest pace in six months, while input cost inflation eased.

Why it matters
Slower overall activity alongside stronger export orders suggests overseas demand is providing some support as manufacturing momentum moderates. Watch whether the next survey shows export strength translating into faster output and new-order growth.

02

India’s emissions growth slows as renewable energy begins replacing coal, UNEP report finds

UNEP’s Emissions Gap Report 2026 finds that India broke from rapid emissions growth in 2025, although datasets disagree on whether emissions rose or declined slightly, Mongabay reports. Renewable electricity generation increased by 98 terawatt-hours in 2025, nearly twice the increase in electricity demand, while coal-fired generation fell by 44 terawatt-hours. Milder weather and slower demand growth also contributed to the slowdown.

Why it matters
Renewable generation growing faster than demand can displace coal output, while weather and grid capacity affect how durable that shift becomes. Watch whether storage and transmission investment supports further coal displacement as electricity demand changes.

Defence ministry signs ₹53,867 crore deals for 8 next-generation Navy corvettes

The defence ministry has signed deals worth ₹53,867 crore for eight next-generation Navy corvettes, Hindustan Times reports. The ships will have advanced missiles, weapons and sensor suites.

Why it matters
Signed contracts move naval procurement into an execution phase, with capability gains dependent on construction and delivery. Watch for shipbuilding milestones and delivery schedules that show when the contracted capability will become operational.

State must use preventive detention powers with caution: SC

The Supreme Court said on Monday that the State must exercise preventive detention powers with utmost caution, circumspection and strict conformity with the law because of their direct impact on personal liberty.

Why it matters
Strict scrutiny of detention powers can constrain arbitrary loss of liberty and strengthen procedural accountability. Watch how subsequent detention orders and judicial reviews apply the court’s insistence on strict legal compliance.

TRAI mandates AI-led complaint centres, 16-hour human support

TRAI has mandated round-the-clock AI-enabled complaint centres and human support for 16 hours daily, Hindustan Times reports. The requirements also include faster appeals and penalties of up to ₹20 lakh for mishandled cases.

Why it matters
Minimum support hours and penalties can strengthen incentives to resolve telecom complaints rather than leave users in automated queues. Watch providers’ implementation of human escalation and the regulator’s reporting of complaint outcomes and penalties.

Razorpay gets final licence to expand cross-border payments from GIFT City

Razorpay has received a final licence to expand cross-border payments from GIFT City. The approval allows foreign currency transfers and multi-currency accounts under a single regulatory framework.

Why it matters
A unified permission framework can reduce regulatory fragmentation for cross-border payment services and account offerings. Watch which authorised services Razorpay makes available and the transaction activity it subsequently reports.

Pronto Founder’s Provision Labs Acquires Hardware Startup Basilisk Industries

Anjali Sardana’s physical AI venture Provision Labs has acquired Mumbai-based hardware startup Basilisk Industries for an undisclosed sum, Inc42 reports.

Why it matters
