01

Odisha scraps pension quota system, adds over 500,000 beneficiaries

Business Standard reports that Odisha has abolished its quota-based pension system, expanding Madhu Babu Pension Yojana coverage to 4.18 million beneficiaries at an additional annual cost of ₹625 crore.

Why it matters
Wider pension coverage can support recipient households while increasing the state's recurring spending commitments. Watch whether the expanded entitlement translates into regular pension payments.

02

Blackstone to invest ₹5,000 crore in IIFL Finance for 16% stake

Mint reports that Blackstone will invest ₹5,000 crore in IIFL Finance for a fully diluted 16% stake as part of a ₹6,414-crore fundraise intended to support secured lending expansion.

Why it matters
Additional equity can increase the lender's capacity to grow without relying entirely on borrowed funding. Watch for completion of the capital raise and subsequent secured-loan growth.

RSS · Mint Companies, 10 Nov · 13:31 UTC

03

Unemployment dips slightly to 5.1% in July-September: Govt survey

Business Standard reports that the PLFS put unemployment among people aged 15 and above at 5.1% in July-September 2026, down from 5.4% in the previous quarter, driven by lower rural joblessness.

Why it matters
A rural-led improvement can strengthen household income prospects while leaving urban labour-market pressures unresolved. Watch whether the next survey shows the improvement extending across rural and urban workers.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 10 Nov · 12:24 UTC

04

Grandson can’t claim birthright in property after partition: Supreme Court

Hindustan Times reports that the Supreme Court dismissed a 29-year-old partition suit, holding that property inherited by a father under the Hindu Succession Act becomes his individual property.

Why it matters
The distinction between individual and ancestral property can determine whether descendants can demand a share by birth. Watch how subsequent partition disputes apply the ruling to the source and character of inherited property.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 10 Nov · 18:49 UTC

05

Society tag can’t shield govt-controlled bodies from constitutional scrutiny: Supreme Court

Hindustan Times reports that a society label cannot shield government-controlled bodies from constitutional scrutiny, while the Supreme Court clarified that government grants or public functions alone do not automatically make an organisation an arm of the State.

Why it matters
The distinction can shape access to public-law remedies against organisations operating under government control. Watch how later cases establish government control rather than relying solely on funding or public functions.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 10 Nov · 08:45 UTC

06

'List Measures To Stop Digital Arrests, Cybercrimes': Top Court To WhatsApp

NDTV reports that the Supreme Court asked WhatsApp to list measures against digital arrests and cybercrimes and counsel Kapil Sibal told the bench that the platform had acted against 9,400 accounts and would file an affidavit detailing preventive steps.

Why it matters
Requiring a documented response can expose the gap between platform enforcement activity and protection against impersonation fraud. Watch for the promised affidavit and the court's assessment of the measures described.

RSS · NDTV India, 10 Nov · 18:53 UTC

07

Regulator caps charges for internet-based links between telecom networks, pushes for phasing out of older port technology

Hindustan Times reports that TRAI has capped charges for internet-based links between telecom networks as part of a push to retire older technology used to connect calls across networks.

Why it matters
Interconnection pricing can influence operators' incentives to migrate call traffic toward newer network links. Watch for operators' migration plans and evidence of adoption of the newer links.
