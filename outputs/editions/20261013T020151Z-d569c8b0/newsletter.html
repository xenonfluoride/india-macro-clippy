01

Food, fuel push retail inflation to 23-month high of 5.54% in September

Business Standard reports September retail inflation of 5.54%, up from 4.83% in August. Food inflation climbed to 7.74%, led by onion prices, with transport and services costs adding pressure.

Why it matters
Higher food and essential-service prices can reduce households' room for discretionary spending. Watch whether subsequent inflation readings show food-price pressure spreading or easing.

02

Tata Power Renewable doubles capacity addition to 419 MW in Q2 FY27

Tata Power Renewable Energy said it added 419 MW of utility-scale generation capacity during July–September 2026, compared with 182 MW a year earlier. The company also reported executing 131.2 MWh of storage projects in the quarter.

Why it matters
Additional generation and storage could improve the supply and flexibility of renewable electricity. Watch utilisation and storage performance to distinguish installed capacity from dependable power delivery.

RSS · Business Standard Companies, 12 Oct · 10:19 UTC

03

Stellantis, Tata Motors PV join hands for vehicle tech, product development

Business Standard reports that Tata Motors PV and Stellantis expanded their technology and product-development partnership. The collaboration will combine Tata Motors' vehicle technology with Stellantis' engineering capabilities to develop India-made vehicles for more than 50 global markets.

Why it matters
Shared engineering could spread development costs across a wider export opportunity for Indian manufacturing. Watch for named vehicle programmes and production schedules before treating market reach as realised exports.

RSS · Business Standard Companies, 12 Oct · 12:02 UTC

04

GE Aerospace to nearly double supplier spend in India by 2030, eyes growing aviation demand

Economic Times reports that GE Aerospace intends to nearly double supplier spending in India by 2030. The announcement accompanied an EY report assessing its contribution to India's aviation, defence, manufacturing and engineering sectors.

Why it matters
A larger sourcing programme could increase demand for local components and engineering services. Watch for supplier contracts and actual spending to show how much of the target becomes procurement.

05

"Unconstitutional": Karnataka Court Quashes ST/SC Quota Exceeding 50% Cap

NDTV reports that a Karnataka court quashed SC/ST reservation exceeding the 50% cap. The court also struck down Section 4 of the Act, covering enhanced reservation in state government services, as unconstitutional for violating Articles 14 and 16.

Why it matters
Invalidating the enhanced quota provisions changes the legal framework for affected public-service applicants. Watch for the government's response and any appeal or revised recruitment instructions.

06

Govt selects 19 fund managers under RDI, committing Rs 7,131 crore

Economic Times reports that the government's Research, Development and Innovation Fund approved its first cohort of 19 second-level fund managers. The cohort carries a total commitment of ₹7,131 crore to help mobilise private investment in research and emerging technologies.

Why it matters
Manager approvals create a route for research capital to reach projects beyond the initial government allocation. Watch actual disbursements and funded projects to distinguish committed capital from research activity.

RSS · Economic Times Tech, 12 Oct · 15:18 UTC

07

HCLTech signs pact with Anthropic to set up dedicated AI business unit

Business Standard reports that HCLTech signed a pact with Anthropic to establish a dedicated AI business unit. HCLTech plans a unit and centre to develop solutions using Claude models and support enterprise adoption.

Why it matters
A dedicated delivery structure could help turn model access into repeatable enterprise services. Watch for the unit's launch and client deployments to test whether the partnership produces operating results.
