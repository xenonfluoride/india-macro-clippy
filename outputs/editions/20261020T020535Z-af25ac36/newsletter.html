01

RBI injects Rs 1 lakh crore as liquidity tightens before festive season

The RBI announced a Rs 1 lakh crore variable-rate repo auction on Monday after the weighted average call rate reached 5.67%, 17 basis points above the 5.50% repo rate, according to Economic Times. The central bank will conduct another Rs 50,000 crore variable-rate repo auction on Wednesday.

Why it matters
Additional short-term repo funding can ease banks’ cash shortages and reduce pressure on overnight borrowing costs. Watch auction allotments and subsequent overnight call rates for evidence that funding pressure is easing.

02

RBI tightens scrutiny of $1 billion-plus overseas investments amid rupee pressure

The RBI is implementing stricter scrutiny of large overseas direct investments by Indian companies, Economic Times reports. Banks must collect and present detailed investment information to the RBI, and its checklist requires due diligence on the rationale for each investment.

Why it matters
More detailed bank checks can add preparation and scrutiny to large overseas transactions by Indian companies. Watch how banks apply the checklist and whether companies report changes in transaction timelines.

RSS · Economic Times Economy, 19 Oct · 19:26 UTC

03

India-NZ FTA in force: Over half of New Zealand’s exports to India now tariff-free, Wellington also eyes Brazil, Canada

The India–New Zealand free trade agreement has been implemented, making more than half of New Zealand’s exports to India tariff-free, Economic Times reports. The agreement will benefit products including sheep meat and wool.

Why it matters
Tariff removal can lower landed costs for qualifying imports and change competition for Indian suppliers. Watch customs implementation and shipment uptake to see whether tariff preferences translate into additional trade.

04

China announces export curbs on key machinery used in India's infrastructure development

China has announced export controls on machinery including tunnel boring machines and related items, walking excavators and specified dredger-related items, according to Economic Times. The restrictions will take effect on November 18, with the report identifying exposure for Indian infrastructure development.

Why it matters
Tighter access to specialised machinery could complicate procurement and schedules for Indian infrastructure projects. Watch equipment sourcing plans and project disclosures for actual delays or substitution before the controls take effect.

05

CISF scraps territorial commands, puts sectors under specialist control

The CISF is replacing territorial supervision with specialist control, Hindustan Times reports. Eight of its eleven supervisory sectors will be realigned by function from November 1, with dedicated commands for power, coal, steel, oil and gas, and ports.

Why it matters
Specialist supervision could align security planning with the distinct operating risks of critical infrastructure sectors. Watch the command transition and sector responsibilities for evidence that the new structure changes operational coordination.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 19 Oct · 03:08 UTC

06

HFCL Q2 results: Profit surges to ₹394 crore as AI data centre demand rises

HFCL reported consolidated profit of Rs 394.22 crore for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, against around Rs 72 crore a year earlier, according to Business Standard. The company attributed the increase to demand for optical fibre cables from AI data centres; consolidated revenue from operations more than doubled to Rs 2,541.79 crore.

Why it matters
Demand for AI infrastructure can reach Indian equipment suppliers through the optical fibre needed to connect data centres. Watch subsequent orders, revenue and margins to see whether the reported demand sustains supplier growth.

RSS · Business Standard Companies, 19 Oct · 08:23 UTC

07

Govt asks Meta to ensure its recommendation systems do not expose users to a limited set of narratives

In an advisory dated October 10, MeitY asked Meta to ensure that its recommendation systems do not expose users to a limited set of narratives, Economic Times reports. The ministry also asked Meta to provide adequate context for content depicting ongoing public events, particularly where authenticity, date, location or circumstances may be unclear to users.

Why it matters
Requests covering recommendations and content context could affect how Indian users encounter and interpret public-event information. Watch Meta’s response and any published implementation measures to distinguish the advisory from demonstrated platform changes.
