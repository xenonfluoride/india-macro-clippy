01

States' combined fiscal deficit seen narrowing to 2.9% of GDP in FY27

PRS Legislative Research says states have budgeted a combined fiscal deficit of 2.9% of GDP in FY27, with nine exceeding the 3% limit. Its assessment flags rising cash transfers and revenue pressures.

Why it matters
Expanding transfers can narrow the room for other state spending when revenue growth fails to keep pace. Watch whether actual receipts and spending keep the states within their budgeted deficit paths.

Sebi aligns margin requirements for agri, non-agri commodity derivatives

Mint reports that SEBI has aligned margin requirements for agricultural and non-agricultural commodity derivatives across volatility categories, effective 1 December.

Why it matters
Aligned collateral treatment can change the funding needed to carry commodity positions across volatility categories. Watch how the revised requirements affect collateral demand when they take effect.

03

Amazon to invest $7 billion in India's renewable energy projects by 2030

Amazon says it plans to invest $7 billion in Indian renewable energy projects by 2030, in addition to $4 billion already contracted. The four newly unveiled wind and solar projects total 934 megawatts and form part of the existing $4 billion contracted through power purchase agreements.

Why it matters
Long-term corporate power purchases can give renewable developers a firmer demand base for financing projects. Watch for commissioning milestones and contracts that turn the additional investment plan into operating capacity.

RSS · Business Standard Companies, 17 Nov · 09:37 UTC

04

IOC gets govt nod to supply sustainable aviation fuel for blending target

IOC chairman A S Sahney says the company has government approvals to commercially supply sustainable aviation fuel from its Panipat refinery. He says the facility can meet India's 1% blending target for international flights in 2027, but did not specify the approved volume or the commercial supply start date. IOC plans to secure airline supply agreements before committing investments to additional projects.

Why it matters
Supply approval can reduce a regulatory barrier to aviation fuel blending while airline contracts determine the case for further capacity. Watch for airline agreements and a confirmed commercial supply start date.

05

GRAP Stage III restrictions revoked in Delhi-NCR as AQI improves to 204

Hindustan Times reports that GRAP Stage III restrictions have been revoked in Delhi-NCR as the AQI improved to 204. CAQM says the decision followed a consistent decline in pollution levels across the national capital.

Why it matters
Rolling back a restriction tier can ease constraints on activity while leaving firms exposed to renewed pollution controls. Watch whether subsequent air-quality readings sustain the rollback or trigger renewed restrictions.

IN-SPACe selects 3 space cos for satellite-based urban settlement mapping

Business Standard reports that IN-SPACe has selected SpacePixxel, KaleidEO and Azista Industries for satellite-based urban settlement mapping. The companies will receive ₹1.46 crore to develop high-resolution settlement layers using Indian satellite data, with pilots in Bengaluru and Shimla.

Why it matters
Satellite settlement layers could give urban planners a more detailed basis for locating development and service gaps. Watch for the pilot outputs and evidence that planning agencies use the resulting maps.

Why it matters
Domestic model serving can widen infrastructure choices for Indian developers evaluating where to run AI workloads. Watch for published service terms and deployment evidence that clarify the platform's practical limits.
