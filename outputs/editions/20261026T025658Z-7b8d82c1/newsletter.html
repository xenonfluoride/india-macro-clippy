01

Govt extends deadlines for cos to file annual financial statements, returns

The corporate affairs ministry has extended the deadline for companies' FY2025-26 annual financial statements to December 31, 2026, and annual returns to January 31, 2027. Its October 24 circular allows the filings without an additional fee after stakeholders reported difficulties submitting them through the MCA21 portal.

Why it matters
The extension can reduce penalty exposure and give companies more time to clear filing backlogs. Watch whether portal submissions improve before the two revised deadlines.

02

India brings back duties on specified Chinese titanium dioxide imports

India has reintroduced anti-dumping duties on specified titanium dioxide imports from China following a Directorate General of Trade Remedies recommendation. Economic Times reports the duties will apply for five years unless revoked or amended, after a finding of dumped prices and material injury to domestic producers.

Why it matters
The duties can protect domestic producers while increasing procurement costs for users reliant on the affected imports. Watch how importers adjust sourcing and whether domestic suppliers can meet the resulting demand.

03

KEC International shares in focus on Monday Oct 26 after ₹2872 cr PGCIL order win; YTD orders mark crosses ₹13.5k cr

KEC International has secured ₹2,872 crore in turnkey orders from Power Grid Corporation for 765 kV transmission lines linked to evacuating 8.5 GW of renewable energy in Gujarat. The orders take its year-to-date intake above ₹13,500 crore, according to Mint.

Why it matters
The contracts can strengthen renewable-power evacuation by adding transmission links that move generation to users. Watch project execution and commissioning milestones to track delivery of the awarded transmission infrastructure.

04

Senco Gold to acquire 68% stake in August Jewellery for ₹68 crore

Senco Gold said it has signed agreements to acquire a 68% stake in August Jewellery, which operates Melorra, for ₹68 crore. The investment will occur in multiple tranches, with completion targeted by February 2027 subject to specified conditions, the company said in a regulatory filing. It said the fresh equity funds are intended for growth, expansion and the discharge of existing liabilities.

Why it matters
The investment can combine Melorra's design and e-commerce capabilities with Senco's existing business while funding August Jewellery. Watch the tranche payments and fulfilment of closing conditions before treating the acquisition as complete.

President Droupadi Murmu gives assent to Frontier Nagaland Territorial Authority Bill

President Droupadi Murmu has given assent to the Frontier Nagaland Territorial Authority Bill. Nagaland chief minister Neiphiu Rio announced this on Sunday and thanked the President in a post on X.

Why it matters
Assent advances the authority's legal foundation, making the details of implementation the next test of the governance change. Watch for published implementation details on the authority's powers, commencement and administrative arrangements.

06

Election Commission orders statewide scrutiny of Uttar Pradesh voter list entries, deletions post-SIR

The Election Commission has ordered statewide scrutiny of Uttar Pradesh voter-list entries and deletions after SIR. Divisional commissioners will supervise the work, while electoral officers must promptly review applications, resolve complaints and remove duplicate entries, according to Hindustan Times.

Why it matters
Supervised scrutiny can improve roll accuracy, with access to complaint resolution central to protecting eligible voters. Watch how electoral officers report complaint disposal, corrections and duplicate removals under the statewide exercise.

The prime minister has inaugurated RBI's ₹169-crore data centre and Cybersecurity Training Institute in Odisha. The government said the data centre will host mission-critical payment and settlement systems, along with other flagship initiatives.
