Wholesale inflation hits series high of 12.29% in Oct on fuel price surge

Business Standard reported that India’s wholesale inflation reached a series high of 12.29% in October. Fuel and power inflation reached 32.84%, even as price pressures in manufactured products eased.

Why it matters
Costlier energy can squeeze manufacturers when they cannot pass higher input costs through to customers. Watch whether fuel and power inflation cools in the next wholesale price release.

02

India's unemployment rate eases to eight-month low of 4.9% in October

The latest Periodic Labour Force Survey put unemployment among people aged 15 and older at 4.9% in October, down from 5% in September. Female labour force participation rose from 34.6% to 35%, while rural unemployment held at 4.2%.

Why it matters
Rising participation means more people are working or seeking work, so the jobless rate alone gives an incomplete picture of labour demand. Track whether female participation stays higher and rural unemployment falls in the next monthly bulletin.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 16 Nov · 11:06 UTC

03

Centre raises export duty on diesel, aviation fuel; petrol unchanged

The Centre raised the special additional excise duty on diesel exports from ₹11.50 to ₹14 per litre and increased the levy on aviation turbine fuel exports to ₹12.50 per litre, Business Standard reported. The petrol export duty was unchanged.

Why it matters
Higher export duties reduce what refiners keep from overseas sales of the affected fuels. Watch whether diesel and jet fuel export volumes ease after the revision.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 17 Nov · 01:59 UTC

04

Hindustan Zinc’s ₹80,000-crore expansion: The big bet to double its metal output and ride India’s infrastructure boom

Hindustan Zinc’s board approved the ₹80,000 crore HZL 2.0 Growth Programme, Mint reported. The programme would add about one million tonnes of annual metal production capacity to the existing 1.1 million tonnes.

Why it matters
If built, the added capacity could give domestic metal buyers more supply options. Watch for project contracts and commissioning dates that show when the new capacity could come online.

05

SC moots aid for families when murder accused are acquitted

The Supreme Court raised to ₹30 lakh the compensation payable to a man who remained in prison for more than seven years after being acquitted of murder, Hindustan Times reported.

Why it matters
A higher award makes extended wrongful detention a financial liability for the authorities responsible. Watch how later courts use this award when assessing compensation for detention after acquittal.

Delhi government allows voter ID, driving licence for citizen services, makes Aadhaar authentication voluntary

Delhi made Aadhaar authentication voluntary for citizen services and allowed voter IDs and driving licences as alternatives, Hindustan Times reported. The IT department must inform Aadhaar holders about alternative ways to establish their identity.

Why it matters
Accepting other forms of ID can keep an Aadhaar authentication problem from blocking access to public services. Check whether online forms and service counters offer the alternative IDs in practice.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 16 Nov · 14:42 UTC

07

Jio vs Airtel vs Vi - which telecom operator had the fewest spam call complaints? What TRAI data says

TRAI said operators’ AI systems flagged 19.9 billion calls and texts as suspected spam in July through September 2026, Mint reported. The regulator received 1.529 million complaints, took enforcement action against more than 124,000 telecom resources and blacklisted 374 senders.

Why it matters
Action against telecom resources and senders can curb repeat traffic after operator systems flag it. Track whether complaint volumes fall in the next report as enforcement continues.
