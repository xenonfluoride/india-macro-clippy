GST registration gets easier as CBIC drops Udyam proof, sets seven-day limit for approvals

The Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs has clarified that businesses no longer need Udyam certificates for GST registration or face rejection because of unpaid bills. Applications with Aadhaar authentication must be processed within seven working days, and tax demands must carry clear justifications.

Why it matters
Simpler documentation and a processing deadline can reduce the time businesses spend obtaining tax registration. Watch whether processing times and rejection reasons change in registration outcomes.

Wheat sowing lags at 11,000 hectare so far amid deficient monsoon; rabi acreage down

Agriculture Ministry data showed wheat sowing reached 11,000 hectares by October 23, against 27,000 hectares a year earlier. The deficient southwest monsoon has raised concerns about soil moisture and irrigation availability, with Punjab and Rajasthan facing particular risks and overall rabi crop coverage also lower.

Why it matters
Limited soil moisture can delay planting and increase reliance on irrigation during the wheat sowing window. Watch subsequent acreage releases to see whether the early planting gap narrows.

UP to begin work on key industrial projects ahead of Assembly elections

Uttar Pradesh's Cabinet has approved four link expressways worth ₹17,542 crore, a robotics park in Greater Noida and GCC projects involving ₹1,710 crore in investments, Business Standard reports.

Why it matters
Approved transport and industrial projects can support construction demand and business capacity as they move into execution. Watch for tender awards and construction starts to show which approvals translate into spending.

SC bars creditors from withdrawing insolvency plan over improved finances

The Supreme Court has ruled that, under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, neither the CoC nor the resolution applicant can withdraw an approved resolution merely because developments have made the transaction less attractive, Mint reports.

Why it matters
Limiting withdrawals based on changed commercial appeal can make approved insolvency transactions more predictable for participants. Watch how subsequent insolvency disputes distinguish reduced attractiveness from other grounds for challenging a plan.

9.97 million names deleted in Karnataka final electoral roll after SIR revision

Karnataka's final electoral roll contains 45,455,689 general electors, against 55,432,314 on June 16 when SIR began, Hindustan Times reports. The reported reduction is 9,976,625 general electors.

Why it matters
The size and composition of the final roll determine who is recorded as eligible to participate in elections. Watch subsequent registration and correction outcomes for evidence of how disputed exclusions are resolved.

Amazon expects India EV fleet to cross 20,000 vehicles over next year

Amazon expects its India electric vehicle fleet to cross 20,000 vehicles over the next year, from more than 15,000 currently, Business Standard reports. It is partnering with Tata Motors to deploy 3,000 electric trucks and boost charging infrastructure.

Why it matters
A larger electric delivery fleet can shift logistics energy demand toward charging and increase the importance of charging availability. Watch actual truck deployments and charging additions against the planned fleet expansion.
