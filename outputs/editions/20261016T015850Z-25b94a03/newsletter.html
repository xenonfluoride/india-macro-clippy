01

India's exports rise 23% to $44.4 bn in September, trade deficit narrows

Government data released on Thursday showed India's September exports rose 23% to $44.4 billion and imports rose 8% to $74.61 billion. The trade deficit was $30.21 billion, compared with $33 billion in September 2025.

Why it matters
A smaller year-on-year goods gap reduces the external funding burden relative to a year earlier, while the remaining deficit leaves India exposed to import costs. Watch whether subsequent goods releases show export growth continuing to outpace import growth.

02

Unemployment rate in country stands at 5% in September, stable compared to August: NSO

The NSO put India's September unemployment rate at 5%, unchanged from August. Rural unemployment was 4.2%, against 4.1% in August, while the urban rate rose from 6.8% to 6.9%.

Why it matters
A stable national jobless rate can conceal different pressures in rural and urban labour markets. Watch the next labour-force release for changes in participation alongside the rural and urban unemployment rates.

RSS · The Hindu Economy, 15 Oct · 17:34 UTC

03

ADB, govt of Japan sign financing pact with AM Green Ammonia

ADB and the government of Japan signed a financing pact with AM Green Ammonia. The deal includes a $100 million JICA-financed loan to help build a 1.1-million-tonne renewable-powered green ammonia facility in Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh.

Why it matters
Project financing can move renewable-powered ammonia capacity closer to construction while leaving execution and buyer demand as separate risks. Watch for construction milestones and disclosed offtake commitments for the Kakinada facility.

RSS · Mint Companies, 15 Oct · 18:12 UTC

04

Supreme Court upholds Bombay HC order quashing Yes Bank’s ₹8,415 cr AT-1 bond write-off

The Supreme Court upheld the Bombay High Court order quashing Yes Bank's ₹8,415 crore AT-1 bond write-off. The dispute concerns whether the bank's administrator had legal authority to cancel the bonds under its reconstruction plan.

Why it matters
Judicial limits on a bank administrator's loss-allocation powers can influence how investors assess bank-capital resolution risk. Watch for subsequent orders or bank disclosures on the treatment of the affected bondholders.

05

ECI directs officers to provide political parties with searchable PDF voter rolls

The Election Commission directed officers to provide recognised political parties with searchable PDF copies of draft and final voter rolls, excluding voters' photographs.

Why it matters
Searchable rolls can lower the effort needed to scrutinise voter-list changes while the photograph exclusion limits the data shared. Watch whether parties receive usable files and report discrepancies through the electoral process.

06

Logitech commences local manufacturing in India to drive agility and AI-era expansion

Logitech has commenced local manufacturing in India. The company said establishing assembly capabilities in India reflects a market-driven strategy to improve operational agility and supply-chain resilience.

Why it matters
Domestic assembly can shorten supply responses and diversify production exposure, although its economic scale depends on the depth of localisation. Watch for disclosed production volumes, local sourcing and the range of products assembled in India.

RSS · The Hindu Technology, 15 Oct · 08:38 UTC

07

Telecom operators to show KYC name of incoming callers from November 1

Telecom operators are to show the KYC name of incoming callers from November 1. The Caller Name Presentation requirement was piloted through much of 2025 within operators' own networks, according to The Hindu.

Why it matters
Displaying subscriber-record names changes caller identification while making the accuracy and privacy of those records more consequential. Watch operators' rollout notices and how they handle incorrect caller names as the November deadline approaches.
