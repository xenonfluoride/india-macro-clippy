India's private sector activity hits five-month high in November: Flash PMI

India’s November flash composite PMI reached 56.3. Manufacturing gained momentum and export orders recovered, while services activity and hiring weakened.

Why it matters
Stronger factory demand can lift the overall reading even when service firms and hiring lose momentum. Watch whether the final November survey confirms the gap between manufacturing and services.

02

India's informal sector rebounds in Q2FY27 led by rural recovery: NSO

NSO reported that informal enterprises rose 9.2% from the previous quarter and employment rose 9.7% in the second quarter of FY27. Rural areas led the rebound after a sharp first-quarter decline.

Why it matters
A recovery among rural informal businesses can support household earnings and spending in local markets. Watch whether rural enterprise and employment gains carry through to the next release.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 23 Nov · 15:08 UTC

03

India-EU trade deal may come into force by mid-2027, says EU Ambassador

EU Ambassador Jean-Eric Paquet said the India–EU trade deal could take effect by mid-2027. The agreement still requires European Parliament approval.

Why it matters
The approval timeline determines when eligible shipments can start claiming the deal’s tariff preferences. Watch for a confirmed signing date and a timetable for the European Parliament vote.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 24 Nov · 02:10 UTC

04

India weighs 20% export duty on select rice varieties to boost domestic supply

The Economic Times reported that India is considering a 20% export duty on select rice varieties to improve domestic supply. The report said rising broken-rice prices have affected poultry and cattle-feed producers.

Why it matters
If imposed, a duty could make exports less attractive and leave more of the covered rice available to domestic buyers. Watch for a formal notification specifying which varieties would face the duty and when it would begin.

Karnataka makes free sanitary napkins mandatory in schools, warns of action for lapses

Karnataka made free biodegradable sanitary napkins mandatory for girls in Classes 6 through 12 in government, aided and private schools.

Why it matters
