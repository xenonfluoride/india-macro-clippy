Core sector growth rises to 7.9%, highest in new series

Core industries output grew 7.9% in September, up from 5.9% in August, according to commerce ministry data reported by Hindustan Times. September recorded the highest growth in the new Index of Core Industries series, which uses 2022-23 as its base year.

Why it matters
Faster core-sector output can improve industrial supply, although the aggregate alone does not establish strength across every industry. Watch the next sector breakdown for whether the acceleration broadens beyond the industries leading September's increase.

El Nino could be unprecedented, may rise to 3.8°C above average between Nov-Jan: WMO

The WMO says El Nino could reach 3.8°C above average between November and January. A December peak is likely, with higher temperatures and weaker rainfall expected in south and southeast Asia, including India.

Why it matters
The forecast points to risks for Indian water availability and crop production if hotter and drier conditions materialise. Watch subsequent rainfall and temperature observations for whether the projected seasonal stress develops in India.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 22 Oct · 02:43 UTC

03

Import window for duty-free raw sugar extended till Dec 31

The government extended the deadline for duty-free imports of 10 lakh tonnes of raw sugar to December 31. Economic Times reports that imports have reached only 3 lakh tonnes amid price-control measures.

Why it matters
A longer import window could ease sugar supply constraints, but limited uptake shows that permission alone may not draw in sufficient cargoes. Watch actual import arrivals for evidence that the extension converts the unused allowance into additional supply.

RSS · Economic Times Economy, 21 Oct · 18:57 UTC

04

NaBFID gets Finance Ministry, RBI nod for national infrastructure project repository

NaBFID has secured finance ministry and RBI approvals to establish a national repository of infrastructure projects. The proposed platform will consolidate project information for lenders and investors, drawing on government agencies including NHAI.

Why it matters
A consolidated project record could reduce information gaps that complicate infrastructure financing decisions. Watch for the repository's launch and data coverage to assess whether the approvals produce a usable financing resource.

05

Courts can’t suggest custodial quizzing, says Supreme Court

The Supreme Court has said that courts cannot suggest custodial interrogation, Hindustan Times reports.

Why it matters
The boundary limits judicial steering of investigative methods while leaving agencies responsible for their interrogation choices. Watch subsequent orders for how courts apply this boundary when considering requests involving interrogation.

06

FSSAI issues notices to Nestlé India, Zydus Wellness over product claims

FSSAI issued notices to Nestlé India seeking scientific evidence for claims about Lactogen Pro 1 and Nan Excella Pro Stage 1. Zydus Wellness also received a notice concerning Complan and said its claims are factual and backed by science.

Why it matters
Requiring scientific substantiation can constrain how nutrition products are marketed to families. Watch the companies' submissions and FSSAI's response for whether the notices lead to changes in product claims.

07

eMudhra security incident: MeitY orders probe, says impact on Indian websites and apps was limited; details here

MeitY ordered a probe into the eMudhra security incident involving eight digital certificates. The ministry said the impact on Indian websites and apps was limited and that domestic digital-signature services were not affected.

Why it matters
Certificate-control weaknesses can undermine website authentication, making the scope of exposure central to the incident response. Watch the probe findings for confirmed misuse and the control changes required of the certificate provider.
