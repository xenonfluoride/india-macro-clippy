01

Wholesale inflation hits series high of 11.4% in September as fuel, food prices surge

Economic Times reports that India's wholesale inflation reached 11.4% in September, up from 9.9% in August. Food inflation climbed to 8.7%, a 23-month high.

Why it matters
Rising wholesale input costs can squeeze producer margins or feed into higher selling prices. Watch subsequent price releases for whether fuel and food pressure spreads further into manufactured goods.

From RBI Action To Strategic Restructuring: Timeline Of IDBI Bank Stake Sale

NDTV reports that the Centre approved IDBI Bank's strategic restructuring, with Fairfax set to acquire a 60.72% controlling stake for ₹52,878 crore.

Why it matters
A change in bank control could reshape governance and credit priorities once the transaction is completed. Watch for regulatory clearances and transaction closing before counting the sale as completed.

03

Hyundai plans ₹4,000 crore investment in Maharashtra, aims to double Pune plant capacity by 2030

Mint reports that Hyundai Motor India plans an additional ₹4,000 crore investment in Maharashtra by 2030. The plan would gradually expand the Pune plant's annual capacity from 1.7 lakh to 3.2 lakh vehicles.

Why it matters
Additional vehicle capacity could increase demand for local components and manufacturing services as the investment is delivered. Watch commissioning milestones and plant output to test how much planned capacity becomes production.

04

Cabinet clears Rs 53,428 crore for social assistance scheme, revises disability pension norms

Economic Times reports that the Union Cabinet approved a ₹53,428 crore outlay to continue the National Social Assistance Programme from 2026-27 to 2030-31 with updated eligibility criteria. Monthly disability-pension assistance will increase with adjustments for age and disability severity.

Why it matters
Changes to eligibility and assistance can alter income support for households covered by the programme. Watch implementation guidance and beneficiary payments to see how the revised pension terms reach households.

RSS · Economic Times Economy, 14 Oct · 12:11 UTC

05

Saudi airport attacks disrupt Indian flights, embassy focuses on diaspora welfare: MEA

Hindustan Times reports that the MEA said attacks on Saudi airports disrupted Indian carriers' flights to Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and Medina. Cancellations were announced until October 17.

Why it matters
Interrupted air links can constrain travel between India and Saudi Arabia and complicate access for the diaspora. Watch airline resumption notices and consular advisories as the announced cancellation period ends.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 14 Oct · 15:11 UTC

06

SC quashes UP police notice asking social media data of journalist who reported Ayodhya temple theft

Hindustan Times reports that the Supreme Court set aside a directive from Uttar Pradesh police seeking the digital footprints of a journalist who reported an Ayodhya temple theft.

Why it matters
Quashing this demand limits police access to the journalist's digital trail through that directive. Watch any further court directions for the scope of protection and the permissible course of the investigation.
