01

West Bengal unveils new investment policy, offers up to 120% assistance

Business Standard reports that West Bengal's new investment policy offers enhanced incentives for priority-sector investments while excluding an estimated ₹50,000 crore backlog of incentives promised under previous regimes.

Why it matters
New incentives could improve project economics, while excluding old promises leaves a separate source of investor uncertainty. Watch for eligibility rules and actual incentive disbursements to show how the policy changes investment decisions.

02

Sebi allows mutual fund schemes to net cash market fund settlements

Mint reports that SEBI now allows mutual fund schemes to net cash-market fund settlements for eligible transactions, while securities must continue to settle on a gross basis.

Why it matters
Netting eligible cash obligations can reduce funding needs without removing the obligation to deliver securities in full. Watch for implementation details defining eligible transactions and how schemes manage the remaining settlement obligations.

RSS · Mint Markets, 05 Nov · 12:36 UTC

03

M&M cuts FY27 tractor industry growth forecast to flat amid drought fears

Business Standard reports that Mahindra and Mahindra has cut its FY27 tractor-industry growth outlook to flat as deficient rainfall weighs on demand in Maharashtra and Karnataka, although northern markets remain resilient.

Why it matters
Weaker farm conditions can delay equipment purchases, making regional demand differences more important for tractor suppliers. Watch subsequent tractor sales and regional demand commentary for evidence that the flat-growth forecast is holding.

Statecraft, law, security, foreign affairs

04

Restore names of Goa family deleted during SIR exercise in 10 days: Bombay high court

Hindustan Times reports that the Bombay High Court directed restoration within ten days of the names of a Goa family deleted during SIR. The court held that an ERONET system freeze could not justify rejecting Form 8 applications and that voters must receive a hearing before removal.

Why it matters
A hearing requirement and limits on technical refusals can give affected voters a procedural route to contest deletion. Watch for compliance with the restoration direction and how electoral officers process applications during system freezes.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 05 Nov · 10:25 UTC

05

SC: Assembly’s remaining tenure no ground to let defection petitions drag

Hindustan Times reports that the Supreme Court said anti-defection pleas must be handled expeditiously and that high courts should not deny directions to speakers merely because of an assembly's remaining tenure.

Why it matters
Rejecting remaining tenure as a reason for inaction can reduce the room for disqualification disputes to outlast meaningful legislative scrutiny. Watch subsequent court directions and speakers' decisions for evidence that pending defection cases move toward resolution.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 05 Nov · 02:08 UTC

06

Cosmoserve unveils Hyderabad facility for space debris removal, refuelling

Business Standard reports that Cosmoserve Space has unveiled Base 1 in Hyderabad for space-debris removal and satellite servicing and raised $6 million in seed funding. The company targets commercial operations by 2028.

Why it matters
A dedicated facility and seed capital can support the development work needed before orbital services become a commercial business. Watch for technical demonstrations and customer commitments that test the path from facility opening to commercial operations.

RSS · Business Standard Companies, 05 Nov · 12:44 UTC

07

Venomics AI raises $2.7 million to discover drugs from animal venom

Business Standard reports that Venomics AI has raised $2.7 million to discover drugs from animal venom. The IISc spinout plans to use the pre-seed funding to expand its Venom Atlas molecular database, strengthen AI-led discovery and laboratory screening, and validate potential drugs.

Why it matters
Combining a larger molecular database with laboratory screening could help turn computational leads into testable drug candidates. Watch for validation results showing which proposed candidates advance beyond database and model predictions.
