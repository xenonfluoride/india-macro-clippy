01

India’s drought-hit maize crunch pits poultry, ethanol makers in ‘me first’ fight

Economic Times reports that drought has reduced maize supply, intensifying competition among poultry producers, starch manufacturers and ethanol plants. Industry bodies have urged the government to prioritise food production over fuel use as maize prices rise.

Why it matters
Competition for the same crop can transmit biofuel demand into poultry feed costs when drought constrains supply. Watch for a government allocation response and whether maize availability improves for feed producers.

02

Bajaj Finance to shrink lending to reckless borrowers

Mint reports that Bajaj Finance is halving the share of new loans to customers with three or more lending relationships to 3.5% by March 2027 amid concerns about indebtedness.

Why it matters
Restricting new lending to customers with multiple lenders can curb overlapping debt exposure while narrowing their access to credit. Watch whether the targeted loan share falls towards 3.5% and how overall new lending responds.

RSS · Mint Companies, 19 Oct · 17:15 UTC

03

CBDT notifies new form for tax exemption claims on govt securities

Business Standard reports that CBDT has notified Form 173A for eligible foreign investors and BIS claiming tax exemptions on government securities. The form requires disclosure of security holdings, interest income and capital gains under the Income-tax Act, 2025.

Why it matters
More detailed disclosures make securities-tax relief depend on a clearer record of the claimant's holdings and income. Watch for filing guidance that clarifies how eligible claimants must supply the required information.

04

‘Citizens can challenge false poll affidavits without ECI sanction’, says Supreme Court

Hindustan Times reports that the Supreme Court has said citizens can challenge false poll affidavits without ECI sanction. The court said concerns about complaints being used to harass elected representatives can be handled under the existing regime.

Why it matters
Allowing challenges without ECI sanction can widen citizen access to scrutiny of elected representatives' disclosures. Watch how subsequent courts apply the holding to complaints and address claims of harassment.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 21 Oct · 01:31 UTC

05

HT exclusive: HAL hands over first 3 HTT-40 trainers to IAF; full training ops may take 2 years

Hindustan Times reports that HAL has handed over the first three HTT-40 trainers to IAF, while full training operations may take two years. The air force ordered 70 basic trainers for ₹6,838 crore three years earlier.

Why it matters
Delivery advances domestic aircraft availability, while the gap to full training operations limits how quickly it can translate into pilot-training capacity. Watch for the next aircraft handovers and milestones that bring the trainers into full training use.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 21 Oct · 01:51 UTC

06

HC: Social media user must get govt order of content blocking

Hindustan Times reports that the Punjab and Haryana High Court has held that users must also receive intimation of a government takedown or blocking order concerning a social media channel or page that is communicated to the platform.

Why it matters
User notification can make government blocking decisions easier for affected account holders to understand and challenge. Watch how authorities and platforms implement delivery of blocking-order information to affected users.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 21 Oct · 02:00 UTC

07

Ammonium nitrate to PETN: CCPA cracks down on 7 online platforms over explosive listings; 3,940 sellers delisted

Mint reports a CCPA crackdown on seven online platforms over explosive listings, with 3,940 sellers delisted. The platforms named by the authority are IndiaMART, TradeIndia, Dial4Trade, Justdial, Sigma-Aldrich India, ExportersIndia and Ibuychemikals.

Why it matters
Seller delistings make marketplace compliance a direct constraint on online access to explosive materials. Watch for platform compliance disclosures and further enforcement orders on the affected listings.
