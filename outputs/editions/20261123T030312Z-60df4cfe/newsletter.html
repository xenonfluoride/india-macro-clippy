Oil, gas import bill up 60% in Oct as Brent surges, LPG use declines

India's net oil and gas import bill rose 60% year on year to $16 billion in October, according to PPAC data reported by Business Standard.

Why it matters
Higher oil prices can widen India's external financing need even when import volumes grow more slowly. Watch whether the next monthly bill eases through lower prices or reduced import volumes.

Engineering exports jump 36% in October, led by strong US shipments

India's engineering exports rose 36% year on year to $12.74 billion in October, driven by strong US demand and higher shipments of metals, electrical machinery and automobiles, EEPC India said.

Why it matters
Stronger engineering shipments can support factory utilisation and export receipts even as the energy import bill rises. Watch whether subsequent engineering shipments sustain the gain across both products and destinations.

Tata Steel’s ₹28,000 crore deal with APL Apollo is part of a two-stage takeover transaction

Tata Steel has agreed to transfer its tubes business for up to ₹11,700 crore and its entire Tata Steel Colors stake for ₹16,300 crore to APL Apollo, Mint reports; Tata Steel will later buy APL Apollo's promoter stake and launch an open offer.

Why it matters
The proposed sequence could reshape control and integration across India's downstream steel businesses. Watch for transaction approvals and the terms of the subsequent promoter-stake purchase and open offer.

Labour ministry launches job-matching portal, occupational shortage index

The labour ministry has launched a job-matching portal and occupational shortage index, Business Standard reports. Why it matters
Connecting vacancies with shortage data could help Indian training programmes respond to employer demand. Watch whether the tools produce usable shortage signals and measurable job placements.
