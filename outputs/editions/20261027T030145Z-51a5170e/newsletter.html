Govt allows flex-fuel vehicles that can run on E100, amends motor vehicle rules

The government has amended motor vehicle rules to allow flex-fuel vehicles that can run on E100. These vehicles are designed to run on mixtures of ethanol and petrol, from pure petrol to E100.

Why it matters
The rule creates a regulatory route for high-ethanol vehicle designs, making compatible vehicles and fuel supply the key adoption constraints. Watch for model certifications and accessible E100 supply before treating the rule as mass-market adoption.

02

Exporters get six more months of risk cover amid West Asia turmoil

The Centre has extended the RELIEF initiative to March 31, 2027, to help exporters facing higher shipping and insurance costs amid West Asia tensions. Eligible exporters can receive 95 percent risk coverage and protection against rising insurance premiums, with claims submitted through the Export Credit Guarantee Corporation's customer portal.

Why it matters
The extension can reduce exporters' exposure to trade-route disruption, although access still depends on eligibility and claims processing. Watch for approved claims and evidence of continued shipment activity to assess how much protection the extension delivers.

03

Tata Motors' bid to takeover Iveco clears 80% acceptance threshold

Tata Motors' bid for Iveco has cleared the 80% acceptance threshold. Based on provisional results, Tata holds 89.42% of Iveco's common share capital, both companies said.

Why it matters
Crossing the acceptance threshold can advance the takeover, while provisional results leave the final transaction outcome to be confirmed. Watch for final acceptance results and formal completion disclosures before treating the bid milestone as a completed acquisition.

04

After spending ₹12,000 crore, only 21 out of 130 cities meet clean air target

A reported analysis of seven years of India's National Clean Air Programme found that states had spent more than ₹12,000 crore, about 72% of ₹16,824 crore. Only 21 of the programme's 130 cities had reduced PM10 levels by 40% by 2025-26, the latest target under the plan.

Why it matters
The gap between money spent and cities meeting the target makes pollution outcomes a more useful test of programme delivery than expenditure alone. Watch for city-level spending and PM10 results to show where additional funding changes outcomes.

05

Drones, artillery guns and helicopters: DAC clears ₹2.12 lakh crore defence buys

The DAC has cleared defence purchases worth ₹2.12 lakh crore covering drones, artillery guns and helicopters. Chaired by Defence Minister Rajnath Singh, the council also granted Acceptance of Necessity for special utility helicopters.

Why it matters
Procurement approvals can create a pipeline for equipment orders, but requirements must still become contracts before suppliers can treat them as booked demand. Watch for tender and contract disclosures that convert these approvals into defined delivery schedules.

06

HCL opens 2 development centres to support Rapidus' chip engineering needs

HCLTech and Japan's Rapidus have inaugurated development centres in Bengaluru and Hubli to support the chipmaker's semiconductor engineering needs, according to a company release. The centres will offer capabilities including mask design, process design kit development and layout design, and HCLTech will work with Rapidus on a pipeline of Indian engineering talent for requirements in Japan.

Why it matters
These centres can deepen India's role in semiconductor engineering by concentrating design capabilities and talent development around a named customer. Watch for engineering programme delivery and the development of the India-to-Japan talent pipeline as the next signs of execution.

07

Tata Communications to invest $400-450 million in India AI fibre network

Tata Communications plans to invest $400 million to $450 million in an India AI fibre network. The company plans a 10,000-kilometre network across key data-centre and AI hubs, with meaningful revenue contribution expected from FY28-29.

Why it matters
Connecting AI and data-centre hubs can turn distributed computing capacity into usable services, making network execution a constraint on the investment case. Watch for commissioned routes and customer traffic before judging whether the planned backbone can deliver its expected revenue contribution.
