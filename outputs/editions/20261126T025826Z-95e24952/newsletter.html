01

US proposes up to 25% tariff on India, offers 17% upon trade deal

The US proposed tariffs of up to 25% on India under its Section 301 probe, with a 17% rate on offer if the two countries reach a trade deal.

Why it matters
The gap between the two rates could change how much US buyers pay for Indian goods and which suppliers they use. Watch for a signed agreement or a final tariff notice that specifies the covered goods and start date.

02

Heavier Make in India push: ₹11,000 crore for infrastructure equipment

India’s ₹11,000 crore equipment scheme will offer sales-linked incentives of 8–13% over five years to support domestic production of construction and infrastructure equipment and components.

Why it matters
Tying support to sales could make it easier for local equipment makers to justify expanding production. Watch for operating guidelines that identify eligible products and the sales thresholds manufacturers must meet.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 25 Nov · 11:05 UTC

03

Net FDI turns negative at USD 4.95 billion in Sep on flow moderation, higher outflows: RBI

RBI data showed India’s net foreign direct investment fell to negative $4.95 billion in September 2026 as gross inflows eased and repatriation rose.

Why it matters
When more investment is repatriated as new inflows slow, less foreign capital stays in India. Watch the next RBI release for whether gross inflows recover or repatriation continues to weigh on net investment.

04

One year since labour reforms introduced, only 3 states notify, enforce rules

A year after India introduced its labour reforms, only three states had notified and enforced rules, Hindustan Times reported. The codes consolidate labour regulations and extend social security, particularly for unorganised workers.

Why it matters
Uneven implementation leaves employers and workers facing different timelines for changes to compliance and social security. Watch which states issue rules and set enforcement dates.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 25 Nov · 12:53 UTC

05

'No reason to interfere with govt decision': Delhi HC refuses to reopen Kartarpur Corridor

The Delhi High Court declined to order the reopening of the Kartarpur Corridor, calling the Centre’s decision to suspend it after the 2025 Pahalgam attack a policy matter.

Why it matters
The ruling leaves renewed pilgrim access dependent on a change in government policy. Watch for an official decision on when pilgrim crossings could resume.

06

Bharti Airtel to invest ₹1,200 crore in three data centres in Assam

Bharti Airtel plans to invest ₹1,200 crore in data centres in Guwahati, Jorhat and Silchar, with a combined capacity of 40 megawatts. Land is available for all three sites and they could be operational by 2028.

Why it matters
The three sites could give customers in the Northeast more options to host workloads closer to where they run. Watch for construction starts and commissioning dates for each site.

RSS · Business Standard Companies, 25 Nov · 16:57 UTC

07

Wipro secures over $500 million contract from energy major BP

Wipro secured a contract worth more than $500 million from BP that spans about seven years. The deal is expected to bring Wipro about $75 million a year and involve the transfer of 3,000 BP employees.

Why it matters
Taking on BP staff alongside the work could help Wipro retain the expertise needed to deliver the contract. Watch for the timing of employee transfers and when Wipro starts recognizing revenue from the work.
