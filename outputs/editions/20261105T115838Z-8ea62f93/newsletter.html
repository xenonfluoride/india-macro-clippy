01

India's services growth accelerates to 55.8 in October, cost pressures ease to six-year low

S&P Global’s October data put the HSBC India Services PMI at 55.8, up from 55.2 in September, marking the fastest activity expansion in four months. Input cost inflation eased to its lowest level in more than six years.

Why it matters
Firmer activity alongside slower cost inflation could give services businesses more room to protect margins. Watch whether the next survey sustains the improvement in activity and input-cost conditions.

02

Union Cabinet approves ₹14,500 crore VGF scheme to develop large scale energy storage in India

The Union Cabinet approved a ₹14,500 crore viability gap funding scheme for large-scale energy storage, The Hindu reports. The scheme is designed to support pumped hydro, battery storage and alternative storage technologies.

Why it matters
Public funding could help storage projects bridge financing gaps and shift electricity availability toward periods of greater need. Watch for project awards and financing commitments that turn the approved scheme into buildable storage capacity.

RSS · The Hindu Economy, 04 Nov · 12:57 UTC

03

Cabinet approves five-year ₹2,814 cr push for critical mineral processing

The Cabinet approved a five-year ₹2,814 crore scheme for critical-mineral processing, Business Standard reports. Implementation is scheduled from 2026-27 to 2030-31, with the aim of strengthening India’s critical-mineral ecosystem.

Why it matters
Support for domestic processing could increase local value addition and reduce exposure to external supply bottlenecks. Watch for implementation terms and sanctioned projects that establish which processing capacity the programme will actually support.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 04 Nov · 09:40 UTC

04

India opens deepwater oil, gas bids, offers to fund up to 50% of well costs

The government is introducing a special OALP round for deepwater and ultra-deepwater exploration, with monthly bidding windows and relaxed fiscal terms. It is preparing to cover up to 50% of eligible exploratory well costs under the ₹84,084 crore Samudra Manthan programme; the Directorate General of Hydrocarbons said bid-round details would be announced shortly.

Why it matters
Sharing exploration risk could draw investment into costly offshore basins, although discoveries and production would remain uncertain. Watch the detailed bid notice for eligible costs, fiscal conditions and the acreage available to explorers.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 05 Nov · 10:10 UTC

05

Supreme Court ends distinction between detention and arrest, says 24-hour clock starts when liberty is curtailed

The Supreme Court said the 24-hour clock starts when liberty is curtailed, Hindustan Times reports. Even a person detained for search and seizure must be produced before a magistrate within that period.

Why it matters
Counting time from the loss of liberty could limit attempts to delay judicial oversight by postponing a formal arrest label. Watch how subsequent court orders apply the clock to disputed detention timelines.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 05 Nov · 06:09 UTC

06

Cert-In issues AI security audit guidelines, sets 4-tier risk framework

CERT-In issued AI security audit guidelines with a four-tier risk framework, Hindustan Times reports. It said requirements for high-risk and critical systems apply uniformly, with no exemptions or relaxed standards for smaller firms or startups.

Why it matters
Uniform requirements could raise the cost of deploying risky AI systems while reducing gaps in safeguards between large and small providers. Watch for audit findings that show how firms classify and address high-risk and critical systems.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 05 Nov · 05:24 UTC

07

altM raises $10.3M in Series A funding led by Rainmatter, Mela Ventures

Bengaluru-based altM raised $10.3 million in a Series A round led by Rainmatter and Mela Ventures, YourStory reports. It plans to expand manufacturing capacity, strengthen research and development, support customer qualification programmes and grow its engineering and commercial teams.

Why it matters
Funding production expansion alongside customer qualification could help convert research into repeatable commercial supply. Watch for manufacturing ramp-up and customer qualification milestones that demonstrate progress beyond the funding announcement.
