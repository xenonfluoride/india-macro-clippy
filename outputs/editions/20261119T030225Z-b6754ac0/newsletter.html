01

ACME takes final investment decision on $3.6 billion Odisha green ammonia project

Mint reports that ACME has taken the final investment decision on its $3.6 billion Odisha green ammonia project. The 760,000-tonne facility is expected to be commissioned by 2029 and supply buyers in India and Japan; Brookfield has committed $600 million to ACME’s green molecule business.

Why it matters
Moving an ammonia project through its investment decision can bring low-carbon fertiliser inputs closer to supply, while leaving construction and commissioning risk ahead. Watch for construction milestones and commissioning updates to test whether the planned supply reaches buyers on schedule.

02

Odisha tables ₹24,150 cr supplementary budget, food subsidies get big share

Business Standard reports that Odisha has tabled a ₹24,150 crore supplementary budget. Allocations include ₹8,389.76 crore for food supplies and ₹5,100 crore for disaster response; the supplementary outlay is nearly 7.8 per cent of the annual budget.

Why it matters
Additional budget provision can support food access and disaster response, with its economic effect depending on subsequent spending. Watch for approval and disbursement records to distinguish the proposed fiscal provision from relief delivered.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 18 Nov · 14:27 UTC

03

Govt imposes 162.5% duty on bulk scotch imports despite UK trade pact

Business Standard reports that India is imposing an effective 162.5% levy on bulk Scotch imports, above the pre-trade pact rate. A customs classification dispute has left over 80 containers stuck at ports.

Why it matters
Classification disputes can raise landed costs and tie up inventory even when a trade pact promises lower tariffs. Watch for a customs clarification or container releases to determine whether the border bottleneck eases.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 18 Nov · 12:29 UTC

04

Centre notifies new prison law for Andaman and Nicobar Islands, bans caste bias, allows open jails

Hindustan Times reports that the Centre has notified a new prison law for Andaman and Nicobar Islands that bans caste bias and allows open jails. The regulation replaces three existing laws, including two dating to the colonial era.

Why it matters
Changing the legal framework can reshape prison administration and custody options, with practical gains depending on implementation. Watch for implementation rules and prison-level compliance evidence to assess whether the new safeguards change conditions.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 18 Nov · 15:15 UTC

05

ICMR launches infectious disease surveillance network for early outbreak warnings

Hindustan Times reports that ICMR has launched an infectious disease surveillance network for early outbreak warnings. It aims to use real-time or near real-time health data for immediate analysis and feedback on investigations of potential outbreaks.

Why it matters
Faster health-data feedback could shorten the interval between an outbreak signal and an epidemiological response. Watch for coverage and response-time reporting to test whether the network improves the speed of outbreak investigation.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 18 Nov · 10:38 UTC

06

Google Begins Rolling Out Personal AI Assistant, Tasks, In India

Inc42 reports that Google has begun rolling out its personal AI assistant Tasks in India. The summary describes the assistant as agentic and says it was formerly called Gemini Sparks.

Why it matters
Local rollout could broaden access to agentic assistance, while useful adoption will depend on reliability and user control. Watch for India availability details and demonstrated task performance to separate rollout access from dependable automation.
