BCGCL begins construction work on ₹25,000 crore coal gasification project

BCGCL, the Coal India and BHEL joint venture, has begun ground construction on its ₹25,000-crore coal gasification project in Odisha, managing director Peeyush Kumar said. He said most tenders had been awarded.

Why it matters
If commissioned, coal-to-chemical capacity could reduce dependence on imported products such as ammonia and methanol. Watch for construction milestones and commissioning dates to test whether awarded tenders translate into operating capacity.

02

India's West Asia oil imports hit pre-war levels; Russian crude falls

India imported 2.8 million barrels a day of West Asian crude in September, representing 54% of purchases, according to ship data reported by Business Standard. Imports of Russian oil fell 14% from the previous month to 1.8 million barrels a day.

Why it matters
The sourcing shift changes India's geographic supply exposure and the balance of procurement options available to refiners. Watch subsequent import data for whether the September mix persists as refiners adjust their sourcing.

03

Sebi eases margin rules for trades post EPI

Sebi has eased margin rules for trades after EPI, Mint reported. Clearing corporations must not collect additional margins from trading or clearing members on clients’ subsequent positions up to the value of accepted EPI.

Why it matters
The relief can reduce additional collateral demands for subsequent positions within the accepted EPI value. Watch how clearing corporations implement the value limit and reflect the relief in member margin requirements.

Govt proposes mandatory automatic emergency braking in new car models from October 2030

The road ministry has proposed automatic emergency braking systems for new car models from October 2030 and for existing models still in production from October 2032, Hindustan Times reported.

Why it matters
The proposed timetable gives carmakers a staged path to integrate braking systems into new designs and continuing models. Watch for final notification and any changes to the two proposed implementation dates.

05

Supreme Court calls for setting human trafficking apart from voluntary sex work

The Supreme Court has called for distinguishing human trafficking from voluntary sex work, Hindustan Times reported. The National Judicial Academy has been asked to frame guidelines for magistrates, and the Centre must submit a fresh compliance report before the next hearing.

Why it matters
Clearer guidance could help magistrates distinguish coercion from voluntary activity when applying legal protections. Watch for the academy's guidelines and the Centre's compliance report at the next hearing.

06

RBI asks banks, payment firms to forensically clear tech environments in case of a data breach

For cases involving a data breach, the RBI has asked financial entities to remove management and administrative interfaces from public internet access, require multi-factor authentication for administrative access, and restrict cloud and backend access on a least-privilege basis, The Economic Times reported. The measures also include auditing for exposed credentials and immediately rotating and purging compromised credentials.

Why it matters
Restricting privileged access and replacing compromised credentials can limit the paths through which a breach spreads into financial systems. Watch how financial entities translate the directions into access controls and breach-response procedures.

RSS · Economic Times Economy, 22 Oct · 19:07 UTC

07

eMudhra to exit website security certification business after security weakness, company says no data breach—All we know

eMudhra will leave the SSL/TLS certificate business after weak keys were found in eight issuing certification authorities, Mint reported. The company said it revoked affected authorities within 14 hours and reported no data breach.

Why it matters
The planned withdrawal could prompt website customers to reassess certificate supply and migration needs. Watch for the withdrawal timetable and customer migration arrangements as the exit proceeds.
