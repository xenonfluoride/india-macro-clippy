01

IIP growth eases to 4-month low in September as mining output contracts

Economic Times reports that September industrial output growth eased to 6.7%, a four-month low, with a high base and weaker mining activity weighing on the reading. Manufacturing output grew 7.6% from a year earlier, while electricity generation also increased.

Why it matters
The divergence suggests that the aggregate slowdown should be read alongside sector performance when assessing industrial demand. Watch the next production release for whether mining recovers while manufacturing maintains its expansion.

02

Cabinet approves ₹10,000 crore scheme to boost container manufacturing

The Cabinet approved a ₹10,000 crore container manufacturing scheme. Business Standard reports that financial support will run until 2039-40, aiming to reduce import dependence, bridge cost gaps and raise annual capacity tenfold to 750,000 TEUs.

Why it matters
Bridging manufacturing cost gaps could make domestic container supply more competitive for India's logistics industry. Watch the support terms and subsequent capacity additions to assess how much of the target becomes usable supply.

RSS · Business Standard Economy & Policy, 28 Oct · 13:12 UTC

03

Government extends PLI scheme for textiles until 2033-34 to boost MSME participation and investments

The government extended the textiles Production Linked Incentive scheme until 2033-34, Economic Times reports. The ministry reduced the minimum turnover requirement for certain manufacturers from ₹200 crore to ₹100 crore, and the reopened application portal will accept submissions from November 1 to December 31.

Why it matters
The lower turnover threshold could bring more textile manufacturers within reach of production incentives. Watch the reopened application window for participation by newly eligible manufacturers and their investment commitments.

RSS · Economic Times Economy, 28 Oct · 20:39 UTC

04

Union cabinet approves 3% DA hike for 11.6 million central employees and pensioners, effective July 1

The Union Cabinet approved a 3% dearness allowance increase for employees and pensioners, retrospectively effective from July 1. Economic Times reports that the increase is expected to benefit over 11.6 million people and cost the exchequer ₹10,016 crore.

Why it matters
The retrospective allowance increase can lift beneficiary household cash flow while adding to government spending. Watch the payment schedule to see when the approved increase and retrospective amounts reach beneficiaries.

RSS · Economic Times Economy, 28 Oct · 20:33 UTC

National & Strategy

Statecraft, law, security, foreign affairs

05

TB Alliance fails to get patent for six-month treatment regimen

The Indian Patent Office rejected a patent application for BPaL on Tuesday, according to a joint statement by the groups that opposed it, reported by Hindustan Times. The report describes BPaL as a three-medicine combination that has cut drug-resistant tuberculosis treatment to six months.

Why it matters
Refusal of this patent application may preserve room for competing supply of the treatment combination. Watch any challenge to the refusal and subsequent supplier announcements for evidence of changes in treatment availability.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 29 Oct · 02:25 UTC

06

UAPA does not bar bail for all foreigners; illegal entry is key, rules Supreme Court

The Supreme Court ruled that the UAPA bail bar does not apply to all foreigners, Hindustan Times reports. The court said the statutory bar applies only when a foreigner has entered India without authorisation or illegally.

Why it matters
Tying this statutory bar to the manner of entry narrows a nationality-based obstacle to bail consideration. Watch how subsequent bail orders apply the entry condition alongside other applicable requirements.

RSS · Hindustan Times India, 28 Oct · 02:50 UTC

07

PhonePe rebrands Indus Appstore, expands into digital goods and webstore

PhonePe is rebranding Indus Appstore and expanding it into digital goods and a webstore, Business Standard reports. The marketplace is present on more than 200 million devices through OEM partnerships and is adding gift cards and top-ups as it seeks wider consumer and developer reach.

Why it matters
Adding digital purchases and a webstore could give the marketplace transaction channels beyond app distribution. Watch whether the expanded offerings convert device presence into repeat consumer transactions and developer participation.
